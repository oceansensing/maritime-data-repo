# maritime-data-repo

The United States' maritime infrastructure, as data for the oceansensing.org
ocean map: the traffic lanes, separation zones, fairways, areas to be avoided
and other routing measures on NOAA's charts; offshore wind leases and the
rescinded wind planning areas; offshore wind turbines and substations;
submarine cable corridors; and the oil and gas pipelines and platforms in the
Gulf of America. None of it is the regulation it maps, and none of it is for
navigation: a regulated area's rule is its text in the Code of Federal
Regulations, and the charts and the Notices to Mariners are what a mariner
navigates by.

Updated daily.
